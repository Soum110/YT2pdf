About YT2PDFS

Building the most precise, effortless tool to transform educational video lectures into publication-grade slide PDFs and AI study companions.

Our Mission

Online video is the greatest repository of human knowledge in history. Universities like MIT, Stanford, IITs, and Harvard publish masterclasses, technical workshops, and cutting-edge research completely free on YouTube. However, studying from video is fundamentally inefficient: you cannot easily search video, copy equations, bookmark pages, or highlight notes.

YT2PDFS was founded to solve this bottleneck. We give learners the power to convert any YouTube lecture link into a pristine presentation slide PDF with one click, while synthesizing structured academic study guides powered by state-of-the-art multimodal AI.

How Our Technology Works

Unlike basic screenshot tools that grab frames on arbitrary timers, YT2PDFS uses a multi-stage intelligent computer vision and AI pipeline:

1. Computer Vision Slide Extraction

Our OpenCV engine compares consecutive video frames using perceptual hashing and histogram difference algorithms. It detects true slide scene transitions while filtering out lecturer webcam overlays, mouse movements, and video playback scrub bars.

2. Interactive Slide Deck Studio

You maintain full control. Our client-side studio allows you to preview all detected slides, zoom in with full-screen lightbox inspection, and delete or toggle duplicate animation frames before compiling your final PDF.

3. Multimodal AI Lecture Notes

By pairing the spoken audio transcript with slide OCR frames, our AI engine synthesizes a comprehensive textbook chapter complete with verified KaTeX LaTeX equations, derivations, step-by-step proofs, and solved practice questions.

4. Zero Storage, Privacy-First

We believe in ethical web software. Video streams are analyzed ephemerally in isolated sandboxes, your downloads are delivered immediately, and no personal accounts or tracking cookies are required.

Our Values

"Education should be accessible, permanent, and structured for active recall."

YT2PDFS is completely free to use online. We do not place intrusive watermarks across your academic slides, lock downloads behind paywalls, or enforce artificial quotas. Whether you are an undergraduate preparing for exams, a researcher cataloging conference presentations, or a software engineer learning a new framework, YT2PDFS is designed for you.
