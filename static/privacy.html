Privacy Policy

We respect your fundamental right to privacy. YT2PDFS is designed from the ground up to operate without collecting personal user profiles or selling data.

Last Updated: September 14, 2026
Core Privacy Guarantee: YT2PDFS does not require account creation, does not track your identity across the web, and deletes all video processing artifacts automatically.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike conventional converters, YT2PDFS adheres to strict data minimization principles:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for your name, phone number, physical address, or credit card details.
  • No Account Sign-Up: You can convert YouTube videos to PDF slide decks and AI lecture notes without creating any account or password.
  • Data Minimization: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or video conversion data to third parties.

2. How Video Processing Operates

When you enter a YouTube link to convert:

  • Ephemeral Stream Ingestion: Our server queries public YouTube streaming feeds to extract video frames and transcripts for the requested job.
  • Server-Side Sandboxing: Slide scene detection, OCR text extraction, and PDF compilation take place inside isolated job directories.
  • Automated Data Expiration: Extracted slide images and compiled PDF files are held ephemerally to allow your browser to download them, after which they are systematically deleted from our servers.

3. Third-Party Integrations

To provide our video to PDF conversion service, we interact with select service providers:

  • YouTube (Google LLC): Video metadata and public stream segments are retrieved using open-source tools. Your interaction with YouTube's public videos remains governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising Partners: We may display advertisements from third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdSense) to support the free operation of our service. These partners may use cookies to serve advertisements based on web activity in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

4. Browser Storage & Preferences

YT2PDFS uses standard browser localStorage solely to remember your chosen user interface theme (Dark Mode or Light Mode). This preference stays on your personal device and is never transmitted to our servers or third parties.

5. Data Security & HTTPS Encryption

All communications between your browser and our server are encrypted via Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). We employ industry-standard security headers to shield against cross-site scripting (XSS) and clickjacking attacks.

6. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Because YT2PDFS does not store personal identifiable information, IP address profiles, or persistent user records, we do not have user databases to query, sell, or rent. However, if you have any questions regarding your digital rights under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), please contact us.

7. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

For questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our Contact Us page.
